*2024-2026, Lexington, KY* <br>
*found and repurposed fabrics* <br>
<br> 
Patchwork Possibilities was a project focused on expanding the stories of community organizing in Kentucky.  Through this project I worked with 10 different organizations, utilizing film photography, printmaking, and quilts to lift up each groups efforts. My goal was to providing quality evergreen materials supporting outreach, narrative development, and base building efforts. With the responsive nature of community organizing in a volatile political climate, we do not always have the time to create more in depth banners, fliers, or other needed items. This project allowed for proactive response to the visual strategy needs of critical organizing in the area.
